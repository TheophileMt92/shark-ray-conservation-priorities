SPECIES DISTRIBUTIONS
Range maps for 1,000 species
Global IUCN range maps of all species with IUCN, FUSE and EDGE2 data, projected onto a 0.5° ocean grid.
PROTECTED AREAS
Marine Protected Areas
Global database of no-take MPAs, areas where all extractive activities are prohibited.
CONSERVATION INDICES
Three complementary indices
Extinction riskIUCN Red List status. How threatened is each species?
Ecological roleFUSE. How unique and specialised is each species' function, weighted by extinction risk?
Evolutionary heritageEDGE2. How ancient and distinct is each species' lineage, weighted by extinction risk?
HUMAN FOOTPRINT
Global fishing pressure
Vessel tracking (AIS) and satellite radar (SAR) combined to estimate industrial fishing effort across ocean regions.
GAP ANALYSIS
Where are the protection gaps?
We calculate how much of each species' range falls within no-take MPAs, and compare this to what would be expected by chance.
CONSERVATION PRIORITISATION
Where should we protect?
Using the CAPTAIN reinforcement learning algorithm, we identify which ocean areas would maximise conservation benefit, separately for each of the three indices, within a 10% strict protection target, while limiting the number of poorly protected species.
CONFLICTS AND OPPORTUNITIES
Where are the conflicts and opportunities?
We overlay conservation priority maps with fishing pressure to identify areas of high conflict, where intervention is most urgent, and areas where proactive protection is possible.
OUTPUT 1
A global picture of protection shortfalls
Species-level and region-level assessment of how poorly current MPAs serve elasmobranch conservation.
OUTPUT 2
Priority maps across three indices
Spatial maps identifying where ocean protection would deliver the greatest benefit for threatened, functionally unique and specialised, and evolutionarily distinct species, and where all three converge.
OUTPUT 3
An evidence base for strategic action
A clear distinction between areas of high conflict requiring intervention and areas suited to proactive protection, informing where and how conservation resources should be deployed.
